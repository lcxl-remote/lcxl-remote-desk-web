# Investigation and reply checklist

- Record the reported platform, affected version, expected behavior and actual
  behavior. Mark missing information explicitly.
- Separate the reporter's statements, directly observed results, and hypotheses.
- For each reproduction or regression check, record the selected environment,
  steps, observed outcome, and relevant limits.
- Keep device details and private findings in the product unless they are
  authorized for the original issue's publication audience.
- A reply can state the supported conclusion, available reproduction steps,
  uncertainty and next useful action. Do not claim a fix or successful test
  without evidence included in the publication workset.
- Do not claim a comment was delivered until its host receipt confirms delivery.
